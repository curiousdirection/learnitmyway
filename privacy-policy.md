# Privacy policy

_Last updated: 10 October 2026_

This blog does not use cookies, does not track you across other websites and does not sell or share personal information.

## What is collected

- **Analytics:** We use [Cloudflare Web Analytics](https://www.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/) to see aggregate statistics such as page views, referrers, browser type and country. It does not use cookies or fingerprinting. See [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).
- **Hosting:** The site is hosted by [Netlify](https://www.netlify.com/), which processes requests (including your IP address) and may keep server logs. See [Netlify's privacy policy](https://www.netlify.com/privacy/).
- **Images:** Some images are served by [Cloudinary](https://cloudinary.com/), so your browser requests them directly from Cloudinary. See [Cloudinary's privacy policy](https://cloudinary.com/privacy).

Links to other websites are not covered by this policy.

## Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please [open an issue](https://github.com/curiousdirection/learnitmyway/issues).

## Changes

Changes to this policy will be published on this page with a new "last updated" date.
